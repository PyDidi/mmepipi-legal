Frequently asked questions

How are toilets scored?

Each place gets a score from real users' reviews, rating cleanliness, supplies and comfort. The map colours pins by their score so you can spot the best ones at a glance.

How do I add a place that's missing?

On the map, tap the add button (bottom-left), drop the pin on the spot, enter the name and cuisine, and submit. New places are checked before they appear for everyone — and you can rate yours straight away.

How do I leave a review?

Open a place on the map or via search and tap to rate it. You can review the same place up to twice per month.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to the Profile tab and choose Delete account. This removes your profile; your reviews are removed or anonymised. You can also email us to request deletion.

The map or my location isn't working

Make sure location permission is enabled for Mme. Pipi in your device settings. If problems persist, email us with your device model and iOS/Android version.
